Teaching Experience

Business Analytics II (GB 307)

University of Wisconsin–Madison, Fall 2026

This course builds students’ understanding and practical skills in data analysis techniques, including regression, time-series forecasting, optimization, and simulation. It emphasizes applying these methods to support organizational decision-making and achieve business objectives. Advanced Econometrics I (Graduate)

Peking University, Fall 2022

This course focuses on the specification and estimation of the linear regression model. Advanced topics include instrumental variables, generalized least squares, maximum likelihood estimation, and discrete choice models. As the first part of a two-semester sequence in advanced econometrics, the course emphasizes theoretical analysis, covering model assumptions, the derivation of coefficient estimators, and statistical inference and prediction.
